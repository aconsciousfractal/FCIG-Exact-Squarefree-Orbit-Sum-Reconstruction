# Public claim boundary

## Supported finite statements

All statements use Boolean subsets of ordered non-loop arcs on `n=4` or
`n=5` labelled vertices, the natural action of `S_n` or `A_n`, and the
complete cumulative dictionary of integer squarefree support-orbit sums.

1. In action order `S4,A4,S5,A5`, the exact least separating degrees are
   `3,3,4,4`.
2. The corresponding dictionary sizes are `19,29,83,124`.
3. Minimum separating subfamilies inside exactly those dictionaries have
   sizes `7,11,14,17`; explicit witnesses and complete lower certificates
   establish equality.
4. One degree below reconstruction, the residual-pair counts are
   `39,51,190,113`, and the counts admitting an individual tree-supported
   top-degree separator are `39,51,190,105`.
5. The eight tree-blind `A5` residual pairs are pairs of `A5` orbits lying in
   one `S5` orbit. This is only a finite index-two group-action statement.
6. If every lower-degree coordinate is retained, the minimum added top-degree
   counts and numbers of optimal subsets are `(2,2)`, `(4,128)`, `(4,2)`,
   and `(4,200)`. This conditional problem is distinct from the global
   minima.
7. The complete four-vertex global-minimum landscapes have `10` and `896`
   members. Their exact product decompositions are `5*2` and `7*128`; the
   `A4` cubic tree-count distribution is `(112,336,336,112)`.
8. On the eight tree-blind `A5` rows, the signed non-tree parent channel has
   a primitive rank-four integral core. Every separating family selects one
   of the two children above parent `00115` and uses at least two non-tree
   split parents.
9. The exchange graph on the `128` conditional cubic `A4` optima is
   explicitly `4(K4 Cartesian Q3)` and carries a free graded `C2^3` action.
   The exchange graph on all `896` global optima is explicitly
   `4(K7 Cartesian K4 Cartesian Q3)`.

## Not claimed

- a minimum among arbitrary invariants, polynomial combinations, or Boolean
  formulas;
- a theorem for loops, multiple arcs, weights, non-squarefree evaluations,
  another carrier, another group action, `n>5`, or all `n`;
- equivalence modulo complementation, arc transpose, or external odd
  relabellings;
- physical chirality, enantiomerism, chemistry, spectroscopy, energetics,
  synthesis, or realizability;
- equality between signed parent channels and the atomic `A5` child
  coordinates, or equality between parent-cover number `2` and global
  minimum `17`;
- a canonical `A4` factorization, a carrier-wide permutation inducing the
  displayed `C2^3` action, or transfer of that action to another `n`;
- novelty, priority, firstness, or completeness of the literature audit;
- independent external reproduction, journal acceptance, archive deposit,
  DOI assignment, or public-release status.

The finite statements are supported by the distributed manuscript and
certificates. Publication decisions are outside the mathematical claim.
