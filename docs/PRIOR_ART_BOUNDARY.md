# Prior-art and attribution boundary

This repository makes no novelty, priority, or firstness claim. Literature is
used for typed context: carrier, action, coordinate family, evaluation domain,
equivalence relation, and conclusion must all match before a theorem can be
treated as an antecedent.

## Source roles

- Pólya supplies the classical finite group-action enumeration framework.
- Mnukhin, Simon, Radcliffe--Scott, and Dufresne et al. supply reconstruction,
  deck, and orbit-sum context on related but not identical carriers.
- Kemper, Dufresne--Jeffries, and Kemper--Lopatin--Reimers supply separating-
  invariant language and general constraints; their polynomial spaces do not
  determine minima in these fixed integer dictionaries.
- Thiéry, Mikkonen, Curticapean--Dell--Marx, Arvind et al., and Böker et al.
  supply adjacent graph-invariant and motif-transform context; their
  coordinates do not supply the exact directed fibres or minima here.
- Kramer--Mesner supplies orbit-incidence matrix vocabulary. The two incidence
  normalizations are related by `|R| A_(R,C)=|C| M_(R,C)` and are not silently
  identified.
- de Bontridder et al. supply classical Test Cover context. The present
  multi-outcome coordinates are stated directly as a hitting set on unordered
  orbit-pair separation masks.

The closest explicitly compared finite separating-invariant result uses the
`S4` action on six unordered edges over `F2`, whereas this paper uses ordered
arcs, integer containment counts, `S4/A4`, and complete cumulative squarefree
dictionaries. The optimization universes differ.

## Audit limit

The source audit is broad but finite. Failure to locate an exact match does not
establish novelty or priority. Future sources can change attribution and
positioning without changing a correctly checked finite certificate.
