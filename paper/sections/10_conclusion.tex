\section{Conclusions within the four- and five-vertex boundary}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The same finite reconstruction problem yields a coherent four-row theorem.
For $S_4,A_4,S_5,A_5$, the exact squarefree orbit-sum degrees are
$3,3,4,4$; the least-degree separating dictionaries have sizes $19,29,83,124$; and
their global minimum fingerprints have sizes $7,11,14,17$.  The four-vertex
lower bounds have short structural decompositions.  The five-vertex lower
bounds have complete portable branch DAGs and explicit equality witnesses.

Tree-supported top-degree coordinates then expose a bounded structural
contrast.  They individually separate every residual pair except eight in
the $A_5$ case; each exception lies across an index-two split, while many other
split pairs remain tree-separated.  This sharpens the finite description
without extending its domain.

The complete four-vertex optimum landscapes add a second layer of structure:
the $10$ and $896$ global minima factor as $5\cdot2$ and $7\cdot128$.
On the $128$ conditional cubic $A_4$ choices, the exchange graph is four
copies of $K_4\mathbin{\square}Q_3$; a free graded $C_2^3$ action explains the
binomial tree-count distribution.  The seven-by-$128$ product decomposition
then identifies the exchange graph on all $896$ global $A_4$ minima as four
copies of $K_7\mathbin{\square}K_4\mathbin{\square}Q_3$.  For $A_5$, the
antisymmetric channel of the eight tree-blind pairs has a primitive rank-four
integral core, and every separating fingerprint must select a child above one
forced non-tree split parent while using at least two such parents overall.
These statements explain parts of the optimum geometry without altering the
central minima.

The paper and atlas form one evidentiary object.  Definitions and readable
proof semantics are in the paper; complete registries, separator masks,
witnesses, DAGs, and independent reconstructions are in the atlas.  The
distributed verifier reconstructs the finite semantics instead of treating
stored hashes or optimizer status as proof.  Its core endpoint is
\texttt{PASS\_ORBIT\_SUM\_PACKAGE\_CORE}.  The resulting claims remain
confined to the four declared actions and coordinate dictionaries.
