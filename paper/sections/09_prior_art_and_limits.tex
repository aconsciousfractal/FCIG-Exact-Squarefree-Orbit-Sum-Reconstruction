The graph-reconstruction work of Dufresne, Jeronimo, Kenkel, Lindo, and
Villamizar \cite{DufresneEtAl2026} concerns an undirected vertex-deletion
setting and supplies boundary context, not an identification of carriers.
Similarly, general reconstruction under group actions
\cite{RadcliffeScott2006} does not determine the selected squarefree
coordinates or their finite hitting-set minima.

The source audit is bounded and typed.  Failure to identify an exact match in
that corpus is not used as a mathematical premise.  All theorems in this paper
remain true or false solely by their stated finite definitions and
certificates; bibliographic scope affects contextual positioning, not the
validity of the enumerations.

\subsection{Model limitations}

The conclusions depend on every item in the following list:
\begin{itemize}
  \item the vertex set has size four or five;
  \item arcs are directed, loops are excluded, and multiplicities are Boolean;
  \item the acting group is exactly $S_n$ or $A_n$ by vertex relabelling;
  \item coordinates are orbit sums of nonempty squarefree arc supports;
  \item the dictionaries are cumulative through the declared support degree;
  \item minimum cardinality is taken among coordinate subfamilies, not among
  arbitrary formulas generated from them;
  \item transposition, complementation, and odd external relabellings are
  controls or diagnostic transformations, not extra equivalences.
\end{itemize}

No computation in the governed experiment uses a vertex count above five.
The data therefore do not support a recurrence or asymptotic law in $n$.
Likewise, the eight $A_5$ tree-blind pairs establish a bounded contrast, not a
general obstruction theorem for alternating groups.  The source-shape census
does not change the global minimum proof, and the conditional extension
counts do not replace the global minima.

The signed-channel proposition is typed at two levels.  Its $35$ columns are
split $S_5$ support parents, whereas the reconstruction dictionary contains
the two atomic $A_5$ child coordinates above each split parent.  The
determinant-one minor is an integral statement about the parent-channel row
lattice; it does not identify parent-cover number two with
$\tau(5,A_5)=17$.  Similarly, the $C_2^3$ action acts on the $128$ minimum
solution sets and need not be induced by one permutation of all $21$ cubic
coordinates.

Neither the signed-channel construction nor the exchange-cube action is
transferred to a different vertex count.  No novelty, priority, or firstness
conclusion is inferred from the bounded source audit.
