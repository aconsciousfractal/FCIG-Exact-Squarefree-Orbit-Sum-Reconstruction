\section*{Data and code availability}
The proof is computer-assisted.  This manuscript gives the mathematical
reduction, explicit upper witnesses, and the local soundness argument for the
lower-certificate checker.  For the five-vertex lower bounds, the distributed
pair cores and complete branch DAGs are essential finite proof objects:
excluding budgets $13$ and $16$ requires checking all $63{,}589$ and
$213{,}648$ reachable nodes, respectively.  The designated
\href{https://github.com/aconsciousfractal/FCIG-Exact-Squarefree-Orbit-Sum-Reconstruction}{companion repository}
for these materials is
\begin{center}
\small
\texttt{https://github.com/aconsciousfractal/FCIG-Exact-}\allowbreak
\texttt{Squarefree-Orbit-Sum-Reconstruction}.
\end{center}
It contains the exact finite certificates and independent replay code
described in Appendix~\ref{app:atlas-replay}.  The optimizer was used only to
discover witnesses and hard pair cores; the accepted lower bounds come from
the checker that reconstructs every separator mask and validates every
reachable DAG transition.  Third-party full texts are not redistributed.

\section*{Acknowledgment of prior frameworks}
The finite-action enumeration framework is classical and is represented here
by P\'olya's work.  The orbit-incidence viewpoint follows the
Kramer--Mesner tradition, while the language of separating invariants and
reconstruction is drawn from the cited invariant-theoretic and graph-
reconstruction literature.  The exact four- and five-vertex numerical
statements are established by the arguments and finite certificates given
here; no novelty, priority, or firstness claim is made.
