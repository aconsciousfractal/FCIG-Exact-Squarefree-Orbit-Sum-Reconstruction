Let $G$ be $S_n$ or $A_n$, acting by vertex relabelling on Boolean subsets
of the $n(n-1)$ directed non-loop arcs.  A squarefree orbit-sum coordinate
counts, inside a directed graph, the members of one $G$-orbit of nonempty arc
supports.  For $(n,G)=(4,S_4),(4,A_4),(5,S_5),(5,A_5)$, we prove that the
least degrees at which the complete cumulative coordinate dictionaries
separate all graph orbits are respectively $3,3,4,4$.  The corresponding
dictionaries contain $19,29,83,124$ coordinates, and their exact minimum
separating subfamilies have sizes $7,11,14,17$.

The four-vertex lower bounds decompose into one forced linear coordinate, a
complete-graph vertex-cover block, and disjoint cubic constraints.  The
five-vertex lower bounds are certified by complete branch DAGs with
$63{,}589$ and $213{,}648$ locally checked nodes, while explicit fingerprints
attain equality.  At the degree immediately below reconstruction, every
residual pair for $S_4$, $A_4$, and $S_5$ has a tree-supported separator.  For
$A_5$, $105$ of $113$ pairs do; the remaining eight are tree-blind pairs, each lying inside one $S_5$
orbit.  The complete four-vertex minimum landscapes have
$10$ and $896$ elements.  The $128$ conditional cubic $A_4$ minima form four
copies of $K_4\mathbin{\square}Q_3$ and carry a free graded $C_2^3$ action;
the exchange graph on all $896$ global $A_4$ minima is therefore
$4(K_7\mathbin{\square}K_4\mathbin{\square}Q_3)$.  On the eight $A_5$
tree-blind pairs, the signed non-tree channel has a primitive rank-four
integral core and forces one of two coordinates above a specified split
parent in every separating fingerprint.  We also distinguish the global
minima from the conditional problem in which all lower-degree coordinates are
retained.  All statements are restricted to $n=4,5$ and to the declared
Boolean, loopless, directed, squarefree model.
